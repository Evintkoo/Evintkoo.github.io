## Overview

Mental health awareness in Indonesia faces a real access problem. Professional help is expensive and stigmatized; online content is either too clinical or culturally detached. PsychIDN is a community initiative that meets people where they are: in Bahasa Indonesia, grounded in everyday context, and built on peer support rather than top-down authority.

The community creates and curates psychology content that's genuinely accessible: it explains concepts clearly, avoids jargon where it isn't needed, and builds a space where people can talk about mental health without shame. The goal isn't to replace professional care. It's to make the conversation normal so people seek it out when they need it.

## Features

- **Accessible Content** — Psychology concepts explained in plain Bahasa Indonesia (anxiety, depression, burnout, attachment), grounded in real life.
- **Peer Support Networks** — Structured peer-to-peer spaces where people can share experiences and support each other, moderated and safe.
- **Culturally Grounded** — Mental health content adapted for Indonesian cultural context: family dynamics, workplace pressure, social expectations.
- **Destigmatization** — Normalizing the mental health conversation through consistent, judgment-free content and community engagement.
